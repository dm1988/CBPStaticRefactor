# App Purpose
CrewBidPro is a specialized logistics and planning tool for commercial flight crews (pilots/flight attendants). Its primary purpose is to transform complex, raw monthly airline "bid packages" into a ranked, actionable schedule that fits the crew member's personal preferences.

# Key features

## Intelligent Bid Planning
- Converting data-heavy airline packages into ranked choices.
## Tenant-Specific Access
- Airline-specific portals (Atlas Air, ABX Air, National Airlines).
## Professional Workspace
- A central hub for managing monthly flight schedules and bidding workflows.
